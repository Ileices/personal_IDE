Origin-Sim (hardware-to-particle) is a new twist, but all the OS signals needed are available in modern platforms (via psutil, py-cpuinfo, GPU APIs).

What Could Derail It? (What to Watch Out For)
Memory leaks or runaway compression: If you don’t throttle how many fractal generations are created per event, you’ll hit hardware caps. (But your bloat/crash protection and 80–90% thresholds handle this.)

Dreamer thread stalling: If not carefully managed, idle dreamer threads may try to mutate glyphs that are actively being used, causing instability. Solution: lock active clusters during training.

User expectation: Lazy users expect “set and forget”—if the system ever asks the user for technical input, the illusion of a living organism is lost. Make all permissions and resource choices AI-guided and default to auto-expand/compress.

# Hypothesis: Planck–RBY Principle — An Observer-Invariant Color Basis for an AE↔C-AE Entity

## 1) Statement

## 2) Formalization

### 2.1 RBY spectral basis

Define three nonnegative, normalized basis functions on visible wavelength (\lambda \in [\lambda_\text{min},\lambda_\text{max}]):
[
\phi_R(\lambda),\ \phi_Y(\lambda),\ \phi_B(\lambda)\ge 0,\quad
\int \phi_k(\lambda) d\lambda = 1,\ k\in{R,Y,B}.
]

A constructive instantiation:

* ( \phi_R ): centered on the red continuum and prominent atomic/molecular bands (\sim 620{-}700,\mathrm{nm}).
* ( \phi_Y ): centered on the solar-like continuum plateau (\sim 560{-}590,\mathrm{nm}) (thermally dominant for G/K dwarfs).
* ( \phi_B ): centered on blue-violet continuum with Balmer-series influence (\sim 430{-}470,\mathrm{nm}).

For a stellar (or scene) SED (S(\lambda)), define **RBY coordinates**:
[
r=\frac{\int S(\lambda)\phi_R(\lambda)d\lambda}{\sum_k \int S(\lambda)\phi_k(\lambda)d\lambda},\quad
y=\frac{\int S(\lambda)\phi_Y(\lambda)d\lambda}{\sum_k \int S(\lambda)\phi_k(\lambda)d\lambda},\quad
b=\frac{\int S(\lambda)\phi_B(\lambda)d\lambda}{\sum_k \int S(\lambda)\phi_k(\lambda)d\lambda},
]
with (r+b+y=1). The triplet ((r,b,y)) is the **RBY glyph** used by the AE↔C-AE system.

### 2.2 Why no green basis

For blackbody-like SEDs (B_\lambda(T)) spanning stellar temperatures, the **chromatic locus** in any trichromatic space is near-one-dimensional. Even when (B_\lambda(T)) peaks near “green” wavelengths, broadband emission across red and blue makes the integrated appearance **white/yellowish**, never **green-dominant**. Therefore, (\phi_G) is **redundant**; the **RBY simplex** spans the locus to first order, with **non-primary hues** arising as mixtures ((r,b,y)) and **higher-order residuals** (see 2.3).

### 2.3 Fractal residuals for non-primaries

Define residual spectrum
[
\epsilon(\lambda) = S(\lambda) - \hat S(\lambda),\quad \hat S(\lambda) = \sum_{k\in{R,Y,B}} w_k, \phi_k(\lambda),\ \ w_k\propto \int S\phi_k.

Iteratively decompose (\epsilon) with contracted, narrower support copies of (\phi_k) (shifted/weighted), yielding a **fractal RBY expansion** that captures violet, cyan, magenta, etc., as **hierarchical mixtures** without a primitive “green” axis.
